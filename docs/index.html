Hushaboom

A noise generator for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. White, pink, and brown noise plus ocean waves, wind, rain, and rustling trees.

Every nature sound is synthesized rather than sampled, so nothing loops and there is no seam to hear. Mix any of the seven sounds together, shape each one with its own controls, and set a sleep timer.
