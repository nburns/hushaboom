Privacy Policy

Effective 14 September 2026

Hushaboom does not collect, transmit, or share any information about you. The app contains no networking code, so there is no server for your data to go to and no third party that receives it.

What Hushaboom does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask for your name, email, or any other personal detail.
  • It does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or attribution software.
  • It does not track you, across this app or any other. It uses no advertising identifier.
  • It does not use the microphone and cannot record. Every sound is generated by the app itself, in real time, on your device.
  • It does not contain third-party code that could collect data on its own.

What is stored on your device

So that the app opens the way you left it, Hushaboom saves your settings locally using the operating system's standard preferences storage. That is:

  • the level of each sound - white, pink, brown, ocean, wind, rain, and trees
  • the master volume
  • the detailed controls for each nature sound, such as ocean wave period, wave height, and tone; wind gustiness and tone; rain intensity, surface, rate, and drop size; and tree breeze, density, and leaf size
  • whether "play in background" is switched on
  • the last time you chose for a "sleep until" timer

This information stays on your device. It is never uploaded, and it is not accessible to the developer. None of it identifies you. Deleting Hushaboom removes all of it.

If you use iCloud backup, these settings may be included in the backup of your device that Apple keeps on your behalf. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Children

Hushaboom collects no information from anyone, including children. There is nothing in the app that gathers personal data regardless of the age of the person using it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in practice.
